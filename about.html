Our Story

Started with a passion for simple, beautiful things.

Verde began as a small studio built around a love for beautiful hair and meaningful connections. Over the years, our little space has grown into a welcoming salon where clients can relax and discover their own style.

We believe great beauty work begins with listening. Instead of following one formula, we take the time to understand your preferences, your routine and the look you want to create.

Beauty with a personal touch.
